            How to play

            Take turns placing the tile shown on the right. Every edge must match the tiles it touches: trail to trail, mesa to mesa, scrub to scrub.

            After placing, you may stand one of your 7 scouts on a trail, mesa, oasis or patch of scrub on that tile, as long as nobody already holds that connected feature.

            • Trail finished at both ends: 1 point per tile.
            • Mesa closed all round: 2 per tile, +2 per spring.
            • Oasis with all 8 neighbours: 9 points.
            • Scrub scores at the end: 3 per finished mesa it touches.
            • Most scouts on a feature wins it; ties both score. Scouts come home when it scores.
            • At the end, unfinished trails score 1 per tile, mesas 1 per tile and spring, oases 1 per tile around them.
            • AI players take their own turns. Easy makes plenty of mistakes; Hard plays to beat the leader.
